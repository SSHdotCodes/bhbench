What this laboratory computes

LIGHT

Every visible pixel integrates a null geodesic through Schwarzschild spacetime. The shadow, photon ring, Einstein lensing, and higher-order disk images emerge from the path equation.

MATTER

The C++ WebAssembly core combines the primary's pseudo-relativistic strong field with pairwise, mass-scaled N-body gravity in the accelerating primary frame. Inside the physical tidal radius, bodies deform into leading and trailing ballistic streams; bound debris must wrap and self-intersect before it can feed a disk.

OBSERVER

Pilot velocity is inertial and capped below light speed. The HUD reports local speed, gravitational lapse, and combined proper-time rate. Visual sizes are amplified so small bodies remain visible.

LIMITS

The central object is non-spinning and uncharged. Companion interactions use softened weak-field gravity outside their horizons—not full multi-metric numerical relativity. Debris streams follow a real-time ballistic/circularization model and the resulting flow is a thin radiating disk; full 3D MHD, nuclear reactions, and numerical-relativity waveforms are outside this model.
